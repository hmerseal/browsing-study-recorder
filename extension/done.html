Or remove it yourself
  1. Click the puzzle piece icon at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Find Browsing Study Recorder and click the three dots next to it.
  3. Click Remove from Chrome, then Remove.

You can also go to chrome://extensions, find Browsing Study Recorder, and click Remove.
