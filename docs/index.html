Browsing Study Recorder: Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Browsing Study Recorder is a research tool used in a study run by Healogix, LLC. It is intended only for people who have agreed to take part in that study.

When the extension records

The extension records data only after you click Start, and it stops when you click Finish. Nothing is recorded before Start or after Finish. At the end of the study you are asked to remove the extension, which deletes everything it stored. It does not record in Incognito windows.

What the extension records

During the session it records:

  • The address and title of each web page you open, and the time you opened it
  • The page you came from
  • The links you click, including the link text and where on the page the link appeared
  • Searches you run on search engines and in site search boxes
  • How far you scroll on each page, and how long each page was visible
  • Internal browser tab numbers, so pages opened in new tabs can be linked to the page they came from
  • The Prolific ID you enter, and your browser's version information

To limit what is collected:

  • On most sites, only the page address is kept. Anything after a "?" or "#" in the address is removed, since that part can contain personal details. Full addresses are kept for Wikipedia and search engine result pages so searches can be studied.
  • Email sites, sign-in pages, Prolific, and the study survey are not recorded. If you visit one, the file notes only the site's name, with no address or page title.
  • The extension never reads passwords, form entries, cookies, saved data, or your earlier browsing history. The only text it reads from a form is the search term in a search box you submit.

Where your data goes

Your data is stored only on your own computer while the session runs. The extension does not send data anywhere. At the end of the session you download the data as a file, and you choose whether to upload it to the study survey. You can open the file in any text editor to see exactly what it contains.

Uploaded files are handled according to the study's consent form and approved research protocol. They are used only for research, and they are not sold or shared for advertising or any other purpose.

Deleting your data

You can delete the recorded session at any time from the completion page using "Delete my session data". Removing the extension from Chrome also deletes all data it stored.
